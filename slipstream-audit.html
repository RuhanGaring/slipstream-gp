Decisions for you

Braking realism

Your friend may be right about the brakes. The car stops about 35 per cent too short: 318 to 87 km/h takes 95 m, against 153 m in a 2026 simulation. So all brake points sit late, even with the coached fastest lap. Fixing it means less downforce and brake force, relearning every circuit, and a different feel for all players, so it is your call.

Real logos and names

Car textures show real sponsors and makers, and some driver names sit close to real people, while the README says everything is fictional. Left unchanged, as they are your choice. The names are quick to change, the liveries are a big job.

Live race on a worker thread

Still your question from earlier. The sim takes about 2.4 ms of each frame. Moving it is a large rewrite with little fps gain.

Fixed

  • Race messages, best laps, the end of a race, retiring, double clicks, a lost second APPLY, one error ending the game.
  • Tyre choice by tap, pad and wheel. AUTO starting tyre, so a wet grid is not on slicks.
  • Championship saves each round and can be continued. Quit asks twice.
  • Overtake mode from lap 2. Safety car no longer penalises passing a car that pitted, and a crash can bring it out.
  • Arcade has no damage by default, as its card says. Ghosts are per handling and qualifying leaves them alone.
  • Hybrid battery, grass that slows a car, wall hits that no longer spin cars, refused overrev downshifts. All 11 circuits re-coached.
  • AI goes round stopped cars, leaves room on lap 1, makes visible mistakes, keeps its mandatory stop. Difficulty steps evened out. Qualifying times use real pace.
  • Pit entries on Bahia, Sierra and Lone Star. Traffic in the pit lane. Overtake zones for city circuits.
  • Graphics: crowd, Hakone sun, Kestrel road, windows, garages, rubber, shade, kerb ends, sparks, tree edges.
  • Sound: engine voices stop, hidden tabs go quiet, audible tyres, off-throttle engine, other cars' hits, barrier grind, crowd reactions, wheel guns, results music, 8 other cars.
  • Phone HUD, touch controls page, results open on your row, contrast fixes, name tags no longer stack.

Not done

  • Phones and weak laptops: no start tier or automatic step-down, car textures still huge on the GPU, shaders no lighter. None of this has been tested on a real phone.
  • Generated circuits are not coached, so Legend there drives about a level down. It would add a minute or two of waiting.
  • Tyre strategy still has one answer. There are no flags, no spoken radio and no per-venue ambience. Stats and records screens are missing.
  • Settings still have no tabs, the gamepad has no settings, and there are no accessibility options beyond pinch zoom.
  • Authored maps are big rings, and city laps miss the Monaco shape. Redrawing needs a relearn per circuit.
  • Coached solo laps came out 0.4 to 0.8 s slower than the previous coaching on Varenna, Sierra, Bahia, Nordwald and Lone Star after the physics changes.
  • Sound changes were checked in code only. Nobody has listened to them.
